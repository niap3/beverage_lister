About this site

This site lets you look up what a bottle costs at a Bevco (KSBC) FL-1 shop in Kerala, by brand name, with every size and the price per litre side by side. It was made so you can check a price quickly on your phone, for example while you wait in the queue.

Where the prices come from

Every price comes from one public document: the Price List of IMFL/BEER/WINE for Warehouses and FL-1 Shops (Tender 2026-27 - Quoted Items) published by Kerala State Beverages (M&M) Corporation Limited.

  • Prices effective from 11 May 2026
  • List printed on 23 September 2026
  • 1,327 brands, 4,397 bottle sizes

Open the original price list (PDF, 1.5 MB)

The price shown is the list’s FL-1 shop price after tax for one bottle. The PDF is read by a script, and every row is checked before it goes on the site: the tax and cess must add up to the printed total, and no row may be missing. A few things on this site are not in the PDF:

  • Price per litre is worked out here from the bottle price and size.
  • Type (whisky, rum and so on) is taken from words in the brand name, because the list has no type column. Anything without a clear word, such as many wines and coolers, is listed as Other.

Photos and descriptions

The price list has no pictures or descriptions, so a few brands borrow them from open sources, always with credit:

  • Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, only under free licences (Creative Commons or public domain). Each one was checked by eye against the list entry before it went up. Photos that show the brand but not the exact variant say so. Most brands have no photo yet.
  • Descriptions come from Wikipedia and describe the brand, not the exact bottle. Sentences about alcohol strength are left out.

If you have taken a photo of a bottle that has none here and are happy to share it under a free licence, please send it through the error report link below.

Price history

Each brand page keeps a table of its price in every list this site has read. It starts with the list from 11 May 2026; older KSBC lists are being added as they are read and checked.

Trending

“Trending this week” ranks brands by how often people on this site opened their page or added them to Compare in the last 7 days. The top three of each type get a badge, like “#1 trending in Whisky”. It reflects what people here are looking at, not what Bevco sells, because Bevco does not publish sales by brand. Only the order is published, never the counts. Visits are counted by GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses or any personal data. The ranking appears once there is enough interest to rank.

How often it is updated

By hand, when KSBC publishes a new price list. There is no fixed schedule, so always check the “Prices as of” date under the search box. If the date looks old, the shop’s current price may be different.

What this site is not

  • It is unofficial. It is not run by, approved by or connected to KSBC (Bevco) or the Government of Kerala.
  • It is for information only. It does not sell, deliver or take orders for alcohol.
  • It has no ads and no affiliate links, and no brand pays to be listed or shown first.
  • If the price list has changed since the date above, a shop may charge a different price. The price at the shop applies.

Age

The legal age to buy or drink alcohol in Kerala is 23, under sections 15A and 15B of the Kerala Abkari Act. This site is meant for adults of that age.
